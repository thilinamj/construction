Frequently Asked Questions

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A residential home typically takes 6-12 months, while commercial projects can range from 12-24 months. We provide detailed timelines during the planning phase and keep you updated throughout the process.

Our free consultation includes an initial project assessment, feasibility analysis, rough cost estimates, timeline projections, and a discussion of your requirements and preferences. We also provide recommendations on materials, design, and approach.

Yes, we provide comprehensive warranties on all our projects. Structural work comes with a 10-year warranty, while finishes and fixtures carry a 2-year warranty. We also offer extended maintenance packages for ongoing support.

We understand that ideas evolve. Minor modifications can be accommodated during construction with minimal impact on timeline and budget. Major changes will be assessed for feasibility, cost implications, and schedule adjustments before implementation.

Safety is our top priority. We implement OSHA-compliant safety protocols, conduct regular safety audits, provide PPE to all workers, employ certified safety officers on every site, and maintain strict adherence to international safety standards. Our safety record is exemplary.
